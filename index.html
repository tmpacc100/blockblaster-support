Sloshbloc — Support

Sloshbloc was previously called Blockwell.

Sloshbloc is a puzzle game built around the Well: the centre of the 8×8 board is an open well. Hang a piece over it and every block over the well pours in, raising the water. Fill the well to the brim and it overflows, clearing the two rows and two columns through it in one splash. Then the well deepens.

How the Well works

  • Drag pieces from the tray onto the board. Fill a row or column to clear it.
  • Pieces may hang over the well. Whatever lands over it falls in and raises the level shown on the well (for example 3/4).
  • At capacity the well overflows and clears its cross. Its capacity then grows: 4, 6, 8, 10, up to 12.
  • Rows and columns through the well are complete with 6 blocks.
  • At least one block of a piece must land on the board; a piece lying entirely inside the well is refused.
  • A run ends when no piece in the tray fits. The longer a run in the endless Well lasts, the more big pieces you get — the same for every player.

The rules are also in the game: gear icon → How the Well works.

Modes

PLAY — the endless Well. Today's Well — a new well layout and the same pieces for everyone each day; 30 trays to set your best of the day and keep your streak. Atlas — 24 stages with their own well layouts and goals, up to three stars each. Classic — the board without the well. Overflowing earns new looks for the well and its water (Wells, on the home screen).

Sloshbloc Plus

An optional one-time purchase that unlocks Atlas stages 13–24, the archive of past daily puzzles and three extra well sets. It sells no gameplay advantage. Buy it from the PLUS button on the home screen. If you reinstall or change devices, open the home screen's gear icon and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account.

Common questions

Does it work offline? Yes. No account is needed and there are no ads.

Can I mute it? Use the sound button on the home screen, or SOUND and HAPTICS in the settings.

Where is progress stored? On this device: scores, the game in progress, Daily and Atlas progress and earned well sets. Removing the app or clearing its data removes them. Sloshbloc Plus can always be restored from the store.

How do I stop usage data? Turn off USAGE DATA in the settings (gear icon). See the Privacy Policy.
