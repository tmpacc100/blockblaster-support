Block Blaster — Support

Block Blaster is a relaxing 8×8 block puzzle. Drag the three pieces from the tray onto the board and fill a full row or column to blast it away.

How to play

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Pieces cannot be rotated.
  • Fill a whole row or column and it clears.
  • Clear on consecutive moves to build a combo — each blast pays more.
  • Clear a line made of a single colour and every block of that colour vanishes from the board ("Color Gone!").
  • Empty the board completely for a bonus and a new skin.
  • The run ends when none of your three pieces fits anywhere.

Common questions

My progress disappeared. The board and high score are stored on the device only. Reinstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Can I turn the sound off? Yes — tap the gear icon at the top right for Sound and Vibration toggles.

Does it work offline? Yes. Only the ads need a connection.
