Blockwell — Support

Blockwell is a relaxed, colorful 8×8 block puzzle. Drag the three pieces onto the board and fill a row or column to clear it. Consecutive clears earn combo bonuses. There is no timer.

How to play

Choose where each piece fits, then drag it from the tray onto the board. A run ends when none of the available pieces fits. Try to beat your locally saved best score.

Autumn Line Challenge

The challenge runs from October 19, 2026 at 00:00 UTC until November 2 at 00:00 UTC (October 19 at 09:00 through November 2 at 09:00 Japan time). Play the amber staircase board with a fixed piece sequence. Clear 30 rows or columns cumulatively in challenge games to earn the local Autumn Star. Practice is available outside the event, without adding event progress.

Common questions

Does it work offline? Yes. No account, advertisements, or purchases are required.

Can I mute it? The sound control on the home screen mutes music and sound effects.

Where is progress stored? Best score, challenge progress, and the mute preference are stored on this device. Removing the app or clearing its data removes local progress. A running board is not a cloud save.
