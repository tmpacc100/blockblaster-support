Blockwell — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes the current Blockwell application (version 1.0.0, build 5). Blockwell is a single-player puzzle that runs offline.

App data

The app does not create accounts, collect personal information, or send game data to a developer-operated server. It does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or use the advertising identifier.

The best score, Autumn Line Challenge progress, and mute setting are stored locally on the device. Removing the app or clearing its data removes this information. These values are not shared with us.

External links and support

Opening a support page uses your browser, whose normal network requests may include technical information such as your IP address. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use those messages to respond to your request.

Children

There is no account registration, in-app chat, advertising, or personal information collection in the game.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
