Sloshbloc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers Sloshbloc for iPhone, iPad and Android, version 1.0.0 (build 8) and later. Sloshbloc is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no advertising.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, the game in progress, Daily and Atlas progress, earned well sets, settings (sound, haptics, usage data) and whether you own Sloshbloc Plus are stored on your device. They are not sent to us. Removing the app or clearing its data removes them.

Analytics, crash reports and session replay

Sloshbloc collects anonymous usage data to understand how the game is played and to improve it — for example, which screens are opened, game results (score, lines cleared, overflows, moves), how pieces are dragged and placed, settings such as sound and haptics, app performance and crash information, and general device information such as device model, OS version, screen size, language, region and time zone. For a sample of sessions we may also record how the game's screens look and where they are tapped (session replay); the game has no text fields, and no personal content is shown on screen.

This data is sent to the following service providers, which process it on our behalf:

The data is associated only with random identifiers generated for the app, not with your name, email address or any account. Google and Microsoft may derive your approximate location (country and city) from the IP address of the connection; we do not keep IP addresses: the app does not send one to Amplitude, and our copies of the data contain none. Session replay is never started on devices set to a region in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or to a European time zone.

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID), we do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Purchases

Sloshbloc Plus is an optional one-time purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. When you buy or restore it, we record the product, its price and currency, and the store's transaction identifier to measure revenue. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us. Whether you own Plus is stored on your device and checked with the store.

Your choices

You can stop all analytics, crash reporting and session replay at any time by turning off USAGE DATA in the settings (the gear icon on the home screen or in a game). While it is off, the app does not start these services at all. To ask us to delete data associated with your device's app identifiers, email us at the address below.

Children

Sloshbloc is not directed at children under 13. It has no account registration, chat, advertising or collection of personal information.

External links and support

Opening a support page or a link from the app uses your browser, whose normal network requests may include technical information such as your IP address. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and the information you choose to send, and use them only to respond.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
