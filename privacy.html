Block Blaster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Block Blaster is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own, and no server of ours that it talks to.

Your high score, chosen skin, sound settings and in-progress board are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google may collect and process a device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), coarse location inferred from IP address, and standard technical information about your device. This happens inside Google's advertising SDK; we never see it.

Google's handling of that data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • iOS: the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" means ads are served without the advertising identifier. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

The game itself remains fully playable either way, and works offline.

Children

Block Blaster is not directed at children under 13. We request only general-audience ad content (rating "G") from AdMob, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
